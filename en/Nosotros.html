Mission

The mission of the Isidro Menendez National Institute in San Miguel is to educate young people committed to social development, utilizing knowledge, teacher training, technological resources, and the practice of values. Its objective is to develop citizens who contribute to the progress of society.

Vision

To be an organized and innovative educational institution par excellence in the eastern region, with planned and efficient administrative management that guarantees the quality of the teaching-learning process. Capable of producing graduates with high professional, technical, and human skills.

Values

The values ​​of the Isidro Menendez National Institute (INIM) in San Miguel focus on educating young people committed to social development, identity, and respect for the homeland, unity, peace, and hope.

  • I respect
  • Solidarity
  • Discipline
  • Innovation
